Riftbound at your LGS

A Tabletop Simulator experience by Amphi

This mod is built to feel like walking into your friendly local game store on Riftbound night — clean playmats, fast loading, intuitive zones, and every convenience you expect when you sit down with real cards.

No clunky menus, no laggy scripts, no missing cards. Everything is up-to-date (including full Spiritforged set), images are crisp, and the table behaves the way the physical game does — because that’s the whole point.

Whether you're teaching a new player, grinding matches for competitive practice, or just hanging out with friends, this is the closest you can get to the real Riftbound experience without leaving home.
